Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Swedle is a free daily puzzle at swedle.com. Swedle is operated by ADYNATO LLC. Questions about this policy can be sent to inquire@adynato.com.

In short: Swedle has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Your game progress stays in your browser.

What stays on your device

Swedle saves your progress in your browser's local storage, under the key swedle-state. It holds:

  • your statistics: games played, games won, current and longest streak, and how many clues your wins took;
  • the date you last finished a puzzle, used to keep your streak;
  • today's game: how many clues you've revealed, whether each clue was a correct guess, a wrong guess or a skip, the answers you guessed wrong, and whether the game is finished.

This data is never sent to ADYNATO LLC or anyone else. It isn't tied to your name, email or any account. Swedle doesn't set cookies. Clearing site data for swedle.com in your browser deletes it, along with your stats and streak. It doesn't sync between browsers or devices.

What Swedle doesn't do

Swedle has no sign-up or login and no analytics or tracking scripts. It shows no ads and has no error-reporting service. Your guesses are checked in your browser, against a puzzle file it loads from swedle.com.

Services involved in loading the site

Swedle is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub receives standard request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. It may keep this information in its logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Swedle's fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them. Google receives your IP address and browser details when it does. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing your result

Sharing is up to you. "Copy Result" puts a short summary on your clipboard, such as the puzzle number, your clue count and colored squares. It doesn't send it anywhere. "Share via X" opens X (formerly Twitter) in a new tab with that summary filled in, and nothing is posted unless you post it. X's own privacy policy covers what happens on X.

Children

Swedle isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

This page will be updated if Swedle's data practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
